# Sync gaps at 0.9.40 (2026-10-20)

Source: a read-only audit of the decompiled game in four areas (player actions and crime, citizens and AI, world state,
investigation and progression), checked against the mod's code at 0.9.40. Game method bodies are stubs in `decompiled/`,
so the game side comes from signatures, fields and `analysis/tools/sodis.py`/`xref.py` caller lookups. "Breakage" is
inferred from what the code does; none of it is seen in game yet. Effort: S = hours, M = about a day, L = several days.

**Checked by hand against the mod code** (grep / read): G1, G2 (no hiding of unstreamed citizens), G5, G7, G8, G9,
G10, G11, G14, G15 (release calls `SetKO(false)`), G17 (switch postfix only within 60 m, no approach resync), G18, G27. The rest
are the audit's findings, with file and method named so they can be checked quickly.

**Correction to SYNC_PRIORITIES.md:** #13 claimed `InterfaceController.AddAwarenessIcon` for the client. It isn't in the
code; only the over-the-head reaction indicator is synced (G14).

## P0: the investigation loop breaks

| # | Gap | What goes wrong | Evidence | Fix idea | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | **A client's hand-in is never submitted on the host** | The client submits a case (`ResolveOptionsController.SubmitCaseButton` / `ActionController.HandInCase`). The host's `submitCase`/`waitForCaseProcessing` objectives never fire, `Case.Resolve` never runs, nobody is paid, and the next record resets the client. | `ObjectiveSync.HandIn` sends only the answers. | G2 | **Phantom citizens near the client** | A citizen whose host copy is far away, but whose client copy is near the client, keeps running the client's AI. | G3 | **Witness answers come from the client's simulation** | "Have you seen this person?" on the client answers from the client's `Human.lastSightings`. Answers are "no", or wrong times and places that become facts. | No hook on `UpdateLastSighting` / `RevealSighting`. | Send the client's question to the host; the host runs `RevealSighting` with the client's body and returns speech and facts. | G4 | **Traces left by citizens exist only on the host** | The client scans the murder weapon or door handle: no killer prints. Footprints are missing; CCTV doesn't show the killer. ## Needs a test before fixing

- Keypad codes (`KeypadController.SubmitCode`) and delayed actions: does the unlock pass the player as actor? If not, `ItemSync`/`DoorSync` drop it.
- A client's thrown object hitting a citizen: damaged once by the forwarded hit and again by the host's physics replay?
- Host-thrown flash bombs: `GadgetSync` forwards client → host only, so they may never go off on the client.
- G23 sentries, G36 side-job rewards.

## Design decisions still open (from SYNC_PRIORITIES.md)

- Known passcodes and passwords: shared between players or per player (affects G20/G21)?
- Police tape and crime scenes: should the client be judged by the host's crime-scene state (G12)?

1. Cheap fixes that unblock the loop: G1, G5, G7, G11, G20, G22, G15.

3. Stealth and crime: G8, G9, G10, G13, G14, G12, G23.
